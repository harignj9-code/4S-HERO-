Official 4-Stream Waste Segregation Protocol

The Four Streams of Malkajgiri

Segregate at source every day. These color mappings must NEVER be interchanged!

GREEN BIN

Wet Waste తడి చెత్త

Biodegradable kitchen scraps, food leftovers, vegetable & fruit peels, egg shells, coffee grounds, garden leaves & pooja flowers.

Destination: Converts into organic compost & biogas.
BLUE BIN

Dry Waste పొడి చెత్త

Clean recyclables: paper, newspapers, delivery cartons, cardboard, PET plastic bottles, drink cans, intact glass jars, tetra paks.

Destination: Material recovery & recycling facilities.
RED BIN

Sanitary Waste శానిటరీ వ్యర్థాలు

Used sanitary napkins, baby & adult diapers, cotton swabs, ear buds, used tissues, bandages. Wrap in paper with a red mark!

Destination: Scientific high-temperature incineration.
BLACK BIN

Special-Care Waste స్పెషల్ కేర్ వ్యర్థాలు

Domestic hazardous: batteries, CFL/tubelights, expired medicines, broken glass shards, syringes/needles, e-waste, paint cans.

Destination: Authorized hazardous treatment & recycling.

LEVEL 1 +10 pts/item

Which Bin?

Identify waste items and choose between Green (Wet), Blue (Dry), Red (Sanitary), and Black (Special Care).

LEVEL 2 Touch & Drag

Drag & Drop Waste

Drag waste objects directly into the correct colored bins. Works with both mouse drag and smartphone touch!

LEVEL 3 10s Timer

Rapid Fire

10 quick classification questions against a ticking countdown clock. Build speed and earn streak multipliers!

LEVEL 4 Inspection

Spot the Mistake

Examine segregated bins. Spot the dangerous or incorrectly placed item and reassign it to its proper stream.
